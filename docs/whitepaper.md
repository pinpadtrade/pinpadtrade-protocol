# Pinpad Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Markets on Solana move faster than any one person can follow. Pinpad exists to make that motion legible: a calm, always-on board that shows what the crowd is boosting, and a single pinned line that belongs to Pinpad itself.

The mission is narrow on purpose. Show real numbers from public sources, mark every unconfirmed fact as pending instead of guessing, and never ask a visitor to connect a wallet, sign a message or trust a claim they cannot verify on-chain.
